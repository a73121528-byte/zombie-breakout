Third-party 3D models in assets/models/
=======================================
All files below were downloaded 2026-10-06 as GLB from poly.pizza's static CDN
(https://static.poly.pizza/<ResourceID>.glb), then processed with
tools/optimize_glb.mjs (gltf-transform 4.5: duplicate-clip removal, "Armature|" prefix
stripped from clip names, dedup/prune, resample, KHR_mesh_quantization).
Geometry, rigs and animations are otherwise unchanged.
